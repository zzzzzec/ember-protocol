# Seven-trader narrative opening

Version 2.1.0 implements the opening with Rin, Nina and original Fence. Ten main texts and six clinic commissions are adapted; the native second main-quest reward unlocks Nina. Six existing offers move between owners without duplicating supply. Established quest and condition IDs, combat rules, real inventory and health are preserved.

Full narrative guidance is stored separately from the spoiler-free player preview under `docs/narrative/`. Later traders, departures, deaths and endings remain design work, not installed features. Native QA uses disposable Development profiles only; Player startup remains manual.
